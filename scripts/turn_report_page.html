      How the times are counted

      Turn time is waiting plus playing: the working hours that pass from the moment a turn reaches a player until they end it, plus any time they spend playing it outside working hours. A turn left overnight costs nothing for the night. Working hours are —.

      Play time runs from pressing Start Turn to the end of the turn, at any hour. It has been recorded since 4 Nov 2026, and turns Claude played are left out of it.

      A turn counts for the workday it ended in, and a workday runs until 9:00 the next one, so a turn finished in the evening or over the weekend belongs to the workday before.

      — turns were begun and finished entirely outside working hours. They count as turns but are left out of the average, the median and the fastest turn.

      Fewest and most turns in a day only look at days when at least one turn was taken.
